About the Project

VirusLoop is more than just a game — it’s the first digital replica of Tashkent in the PvPvE genre. We’re building a platform where brands can become part of life in a virtual metropolis.

Audience

More than 500,000+ potential players from Uzbekistan and the CIS during the first quarter after launch, aged 14–35.

Integrations

Banner placements, branded buildings, vehicles (Damas, Nexia), and skins.

Our Capabilities

  • Branding of in-game locations (stores, gas stations, billboards, and bus stops throughout central Tashkent).
  • Sponsored tournaments and events.
  • Product placement of survival items.
